What Does the Lord Require?
 
“He has shown you, O mortal, what is good. And what does the LORD require of you? To act justly and to love mercy and to walk humbly with your God.”
Micah 6:8 (NIV)
God’s people were asking what offering would please Him. Through the prophet Micah, God answered: it isn’t about grand gestures. It’s about how we live every day.
1
Act Justly
Do what is right, even when no one is watching. Be honest in your work, fair in your dealings and a man of your word at home, on the job and in the community.
2
Love Mercy
Don’t just show mercy — love it. Give grace the way God has given it to you, like our brothers serving homeless neighbors downtown and asking, “How can I pray for you?”
3
Walk Humbly with Your God
Stay close to Him and let Him lead. Humility means depending on God daily through prayer and His Word, not on our own strength.
 
Apply It This Week
Choose one act of mercy this week: pray with someone, help a neighbor in need or reach out to a brother who’s struggling.
