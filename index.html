SYRIA & ENGLAND OUTREACH
📅 August 17th – September 7th
 
The Lord calls, and so I go to exalt the Prince of Peace in another war zone. There was another bombing last night in Damascus, but still, I am going with great confidence in our great God as we travel to various places in Syria. We will conduct several ministry and evangelistic events calling the lost to Christ and ministering to the Lord's people. We will have major food distributions for the needy in devastated areas, and major Bible distributions.

ENGLAND: I will continue helping a London church that has faithfully persisted with weekly street evangelism that was started my last time while in the UK. Because of the influx of new believers and people coming from these efforts, a new English service was also started at this Brazilian church!

DISCIPLESHIP CONTINUES:
Discipleship is still occurring in Sudan, India, and Tanzania. We are truly making disciples of Christ in the nations!

We also continue to feed the critically starving that cannot feed themselves, and without our help, they would not survive.

THANK YOU: Without your prayers and support, all that we are doing together around the world would not be possible. We won't fully know the impact until we reach Heaven one glorious day. Thank you for your continued prayers and support, and please pray with me for another glorious harvest where so few are willing to go for our returning King!!

SUPPORT THE EFL BENEVOLENCE FUND

 

The EFL Benevolence Fund exists to extend compassion and restoration to men in moments of real crisis. Through your generosity, we are able to meet immediate needs—providing financial relief, spiritual encouragement, and practical assistance that point directly to the peace and hope found in Christ.

Each gift becomes more than a contribution—it's a lifeline that demonstrates the tangible love of God in action. By giving generously and stewarding faithfully, you become part of a story of redemption and renewal. Together, we can lift men out of hardship, restore dignity, and watch God move powerfully in their lives.
