A Story From Waikīkī
Kindness Crosses Every Language

A little reminder that kindness and respect still exist in this world! 🌺

One evening while walking along Waikīkī Beach with my flashlight, I noticed something in the water about knee-deep. As I got closer, I realized it was a phone inside a waterproof pouch.

The phone started ringing, but everything on the screen was in Japanese. I managed to call the number displayed, and a young woman answered. The only problem was that her English was about as good as my Japanese! 😂

Her boyfriend tried communicating with me as well, and after a little back and forth, they eventually found a Japanese woman who spoke English and helped us figure everything out.

We arranged to meet on the beach near Duke’s, and what happened next really made my night.

These two young adults were incredibly kind, appreciative, and respectful. They bowed several times, gave me hugs, and couldn’t thank me enough. Before leaving, they handed me a small envelope with the words “For Me” written on it.

I politely told them it wasn’t necessary, but they insisted. We took a few selfies together, exchanged smiles, and went our separate ways.

Later that evening, I opened the envelope and discovered they had put $40 inside.

The money wasn’t what made this experience special. It was their genuine gratitude, humility, and respect.

In a world where we hear so much negativity about people, especially younger generations, it was refreshing to meet two young adults who showed such kindness and appreciation.

I didn’t return that phone expecting anything in return. It was simply the right thing to do. But their reaction turned a small act of kindness into a memory I’ll carry with me for a long time.

Sometimes the smallest moments remind us that there are still a lot of good people in this world. Kindness crosses every language, culture, and generation.

And sometimes, all it takes is doing the right thing when nobody is watching.

What a special little memory from Waikīkī! Aloha! 🤙🌺

— Chris Tyler

Verse of the Week
 
“Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the LORD your God will be with you wherever you go.”
Joshua 1:9 (NIV)
Moses was gone, and Joshua was called to lead God’s people into a land full of giants. Before he took a single step, God gave him a command and a promise: be strong, be courageous and remember who goes with you.
What This Means for Us
God didn’t suggest courage; He commanded it. Courage isn’t the absence of fear. It’s choosing to obey God and step forward anyway, at home, at work and in your faith. Fear and discouragement will always have something to say, but when they speak, answer with God’s Word and prayer, and let faith, not worry, decide your next step. Our strength doesn’t come from ourselves. It comes from His promise that the Lord is with us wherever we go. You never face your battles alone, and neither does your brother.
 
Apply It This Week
Name the one thing you’ve been afraid to face. Pray Joshua 1:9 over it every morning this week, share it with a brother and take one courageous step forward.

“How Can I Pray for You?” Initiative
Carrying the Light Downtown
  ✦  
Through the How Can I Pray for You? Initiative, EFL brothers head into downtown Dallas to carry the light of Christ to our homeless neighbors. We hand out care packages and ask one simple question: “How can I pray for you?”

This ministry continues to touch our hearts. It is a true privilege to serve our brothers and sisters in their time of need: to pray with them, listen to their stories and remind them that they are seen and loved by God. Our prayer is that each person we meet will come to love Him, join us in learning His ways and pass His love on to the next person.

Thank you to every brother who gave his time, his prayers and his heart.
